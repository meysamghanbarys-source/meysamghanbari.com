# meysamghanbari.com — Website v2

Official bilingual research website for Meysam Ghanbari.

## Positioning

The site is intentionally written in formal third-person voice, as an official research website rather than a first-person diary or student profile.

Primary identity:

**Researcher in Optical, Satellite & Advanced Communication Systems**

## Main sections

- Home
- Research
- Projects
- Publications
- Videos & Talks
- Insights
- Upcoming
- Media
- About
- Contact
- Persian equivalents under `/fa/`

## Content sources for this version

The research profile, technical expertise, projects and publication records were organized from the supplied CV. Website publication summaries are concise editorial descriptions and are deliberately conservative where the final paper text/abstract was not supplied.
